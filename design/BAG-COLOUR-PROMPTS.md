# Naye bags: doosre rangon ki tasveerein (12 prompts)

Har bag ki abhi ek rang ki photo site par hai. Rang badalne ka option tab chalega jab har rang ki apni photo ho. Har photo aate hi main us rang ka swatch aur variant add kar dunga, aur rang badalne par photo badlegi.

**Tareeqa (har prompt ke liye):**
- **Reference image:** usi bag ki maujooda photo (neeche har bag ke saath file ka naam likha hai). Tool mein "Edit / Image-to-image" ho to wohi use karein, taake shape, angle, roshni aur hardware bilkul same rahein.
- **Size:** woh hi jo reference ka hai (square ya portrait, dono chalenge; main 4:5 bana dunga).
- **File ka naam** bilkul wahi rakhein jo likha hai.
- **Check:** koi logo, likhai ya monogram na ho; hardware sunehra hi rahe.

Rang site ke maujooda palette se liye gaye hain (wahi hex codes jo baaki bags par hain), taake filters mein sab ek jaise rang ek saath aayein. Agar aap koi aur rang chahte hain to bata dein.
