# SUPERMIMIC: 8 naye original bags (prompts + listing details)

Har bag apna original design hai aur us par SUPERMIMIC ka sunehra **S** signature hai. Kisi brand ki shape, taala, silai ya logo ki copy nahi hai.

## Banwane ka tareeqa
- **Reference image:** koi nahi lagani. Purane branded bags reference mein mat lagayein, warna tool unki shape copy kar dega.
- **Size:** 4:5 portrait (kam az kam 1600 × 2000), ya square. Main site ke liye 4:5 bana dunga.
- **File ka naam** bilkul wahi rakhein jo har bag ke saath likha hai.
- Har prompt ke aakhir mein **Studio** wali line zaroor shamil hai, taake sab bags ki tasveerein site ke maujooda bags jaisi lagein.
- **Bhejne se pehle check karein:** koi logo, likhai ya monogram na ho (sirf S); aur bag dekh kar kisi mashhoor brand ka bag yaad na aaye.

**Negative prompt** (agar alag box ho, har prompt ke saath yahi):
```
logo, monogram, brand name, text, letters other than a single S, padlock, hanging key, clochette, belt straps across the flap, diamond cannage quilting, letter charms, piston clasp, perforated pattern, saddle shape, watermark
```

## Tasveerein aane ke baad main kya karunga
1. Har tasveer check karunga: logo nahi, S saaf, aur kisi brand ki copy nahi lagni chahiye.
2. Site ke liye 4:5 WebP banaunga aur upar diye naam se product add karunga, saath mein description, details, dimensions, wazan aur dekh-bhaal.
3. Doosre rang chahiye to `BAG-COLOUR-PROMPTS.md` wale tareeqe se har bag ke rangon ke prompts bhi likh dunga.

Dimensions, wazan aur qeemat pehle andaze par honge. Supplier se asli naap mil jayein to bata dein, main badal dunga.
