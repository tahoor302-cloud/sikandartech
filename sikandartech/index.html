About Us

Your Trusted Partner in China

Sikandar Tech is run by a sourcing professional based in Guangzhou, China, originally from Karachi, Pakistan. For more than 20 years we have helped importers, wholesalers, and online sellers buy the latest technology and gadgets directly from manufacturers.

We visit the markets and factories, negotiate prices, inspect quality before shipment, and handle logistics end to end, so you get genuine products at factory prices without the risk.
