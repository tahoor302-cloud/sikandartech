Shipping & Returns

SikandarTech supplies products on quotation. Shipping costs, delivery times and after-sales terms are agreed in writing for each order before you pay.

How an order works

  • Send your product list and quantities on WhatsApp or through the contact form.
  • We reply with factory options, price, minimum order quantity, lead time and shipping cost to your country.
  • Production starts once you confirm the quotation and the agreed payment terms.
  • Goods are inspected before they leave the factory and you receive a photo or video report.

Shipping methods

  • Express courier, air freight and sea freight, including consolidation of several suppliers into one shipment.
  • Door-to-door delivery is available to most countries. The method and the cost are stated in your quotation.
  • Delivery times depend on the method and the destination and are given in your quotation.
